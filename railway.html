Rail Sub-Specialities

Where OPEX goes
deep in rail

Beyond programme-level advisory, OPEX brings hands-on engineering depth to two disciplines that shape passenger experience and vehicle reliability: seating systems and onboard electrical architecture.

Seating Systems

End-to-end consulting on passenger seating — from concept and supplier selection through to fleet-wide rollout. OPEX balances comfort, capacity, weight, and certification requirements so seating programmes stay on schedule and on budget.

  • Ergonomics & passenger comfort standards
  • Fire & crash safety compliance (EN 45545 / EN 12663)
  • Supplier selection, qualification & audits
  • Capacity vs. comfort layout optimisation
  • Modular platform strategy across fleets
  • Weight & cost-down engineering

Electrical Systems

Advisory on onboard electrical architecture — power distribution, wiring integration, and passenger-facing systems — helping operators and manufacturers deliver reliable, compliant, and energy-efficient rolling stock.

  • Onboard power & auxiliary architecture
  • Wiring harness design & integration
  • EMC & EN 50121 compliance
  • Lighting & passenger information systems
  • Energy efficiency & obsolescence management
  • Integration with train control & signalling

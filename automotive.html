Engineering Depth

Rigour that runs

Automotive programmes live or die on details most consultants never touch — wiring harness routing, seat frame tolerances, supplier PPAP packages, line-side quality gates. OPEX works at that level, translating shop-floor and bench-level reality into decisions leadership can act on with confidence.

Automotive Sub-Specialities

Where OPEX goes
deep in automotive

Four disciplines that shape whether an automotive programme launches on time, on cost, and on quality — electronics, seating, manufacturing, and supply chain.

Electronics & Electrical Systems

Vehicle E/E architecture consulting — from wiring harness design to ECU integration — helping teams manage growing electrical complexity without losing sight of cost and manufacturability.

  • E/E architecture & wiring harness design
  • ECU integration & sensor networks
  • ISO 26262 functional safety alignment
  • EMC & regulatory compliance
  • ADAS & connectivity system integration
  • Cost & complexity reduction across variants

Seating Systems

Seat design and programme consulting across trims and platforms — balancing occupant comfort, crash safety, and the cost pressure that defines automotive sourcing.

  • Ergonomics & occupant comfort standards
  • Crash safety compliance (FMVSS 207 / ECE R17)
  • Supplier qualification & cost negotiation
  • Platform-sharing & modularity strategy
  • Trim-level variant management
  • Weight & cost optimisation

Manufacturing & Quality

Production system design and quality management for automotive plants — built to hold up under IATF audits and high-volume launch pressure alike.

  • Line balancing & takt time optimisation
  • IATF 16949 quality management alignment
  • PPAP & launch readiness reviews
  • Defect reduction & root cause analysis
  • Standard work & operator training
  • Continuous improvement (Kaizen) programmes

Supply Chain & Sourcing

Supplier risk management and sourcing strategy across tier-1 and tier-2 partners, built to protect programmes from the disruptions that hit automotive supply chains hardest.

  • Supplier risk assessment & qualification
  • Tier-1 / Tier-2 sourcing strategy
  • Logistics & inventory optimisation
  • Dual-sourcing & resilience planning
  • Cost negotiation & should-cost modelling
  • Supplier performance scorecards
